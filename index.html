Full-Duplex Speech Models Take the Floor When Asked, Not When Needed, arXiv 2026, under review

L. Peng, B. Nuchged, K. Fu, Y. Yao

Full-duplex speech models need to actively offer help to users, which matters for home assistants, tutoring, and virtual interviews. However, today's models almost never speak up when needed. They answer questions and talk after pauses, but a hazard or a false claim draws no more speech than an ordinary sentence.

arXiv · code

Listen, Diagnose, and Interrupt: Coupling Mispronunciation Diagnostics into a Full-Duplex Speech LLM, preprint 2026, under review

L. Peng, B. Nuchged, K. Fu, Y. Yao, J. Zhang

This full-duplex language-learning tutor feeds a WavLM detector's mispronunciation diagnosis into a frozen 11B full-duplex model (VoiceChat) through a zero-initialized residual channel. It keeps the detector's accuracy, with sentence-level F1 nearly unchanged, and works in full-duplex mode: the correction starts a median 516 ms after the mispronounced word, while the learner is still talking.
